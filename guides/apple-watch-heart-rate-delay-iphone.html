Why your iPhone heart rate lags your Apple Watch

By Jaron, developer of Zone 2 Training Coach · Updated September 2026

Your watch face updates your heart rate constantly. Open almost any iPhone app, even Apple's Health app, and the number is often a few minutes old. I measured why while building a heart rate app on an Apple Watch Ultra 4.

What I found

  1. At rest, the Watch shows heart rate constantly but saves it only every few minutes. In my logs, saved readings were about 5 minutes apart (for example 19:56, then 20:01). Apps can only read saved readings.
  2. The iPhone only gets those readings when the Watch syncs. Opening or switching back to an app on the iPhone often brings in a new batch, which is why closing and reopening an app sometimes "catches up". Pull-to-refresh inside an app usually doesn't trigger a sync.
  3. Opening the Heart Rate app on the Watch takes a fresh reading and syncs it right away.
  4. During a workout, everything changes: readings come every few seconds.

How to see live heart rate on your iPhone

  • Start a workout. Workout apps that mirror to the iPhone show your heart rate every few seconds.
  • Keep an app open on the Watch. A Watch app that's on screen can send readings straight to its iPhone app.
  • Keep that app on screen longer: Watch app → General → Return to Clock → choose the app → Custom → After 1 hour. Otherwise the Watch goes back to the watch face after 2 minutes.

These observations come from my own testing on one Apple Watch Ultra 4 with watchOS 27. Apple controls how often the Watch saves and syncs, and it can differ between models and software versions.
